Job Bot Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

What Job Bot is

Job Bot is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer to keep track of job applications. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the user's permission, Job Bot uses the Gmail API with read-only access (gmail.readonly) to read email messages related to job applications, such as replies and status updates from employers. It cannot send, delete or change any email.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is used only to track the status of the user's own job applications.
  • All data is processed and stored locally on the user's own computer.
  • Email data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.
  • Email data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Job Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing Job Bot's data folder from the computer.
